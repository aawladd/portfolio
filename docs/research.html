Research

Physics-informed and operator-learning methods for the modelling, control and inverse analysis of nonlinear systems governed by partial differential equations.

Research Interests

Scientific machine learning
Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), DeepONet and Fourier neural operators, hybrid numerical–learning methods.
Learning-based control
PDE-constrained control, deep reinforcement learning, control barrier functions for safe control.
Inverse problems
Parameter estimation, identifiability, uncertainty quantification, inference from sparse data.
Embedded systems and robotics
On-device deployment of scientific ML models for control and biomedical screening; vision-based teleoperation.
Computational modelling
CFD and heat transfer, atmospheric and environmental simulation.

Current Research

Deep Reinforcement Operator Learning for Dynamic Control Mar 2025 – present Institute for Advanced Research, United International University · PI: Dr. Mahtab Uddin
  • Developed a physics-informed dual-critic PPO controller with a DeepONet surrogate for river-pollution control. Compared with standard PPO, it reduced integrated pollutant exceedance by 11.65% and violation duration by 10.85%, with a surrogate relative L2 error of 2.21% [J1].
  • Extended the approach to safe control with Fourier neural operators and control barrier functions for battery thermal-runaway cooling [U3].
  • Contributed to DeepONet-guided reinforcement-learning stabilisation of the chaotic Kuramoto–Sivashinsky equation [J2].
Uncertainty-aware inverse parameter estimation Identifiability-aware, uncertainty-weighted PINNs for advection–diffusion–reaction systems
Recovering physical parameters from sparse, noisy observations while accounting for which parameters the data can actually identify [U2].

Other Research Projects

Vision-based robotic teleoperationongoing
Designed and assembled a 6-DOF aluminium manipulator (high-torque servos, ESP32 controller, PCA9685 PWM driver over I2C). I am now developing stereo-camera hand tracking on an RDK X5 edge-AI computer for learning-based teleoperation.
Embedded biomedical AI
Evaluated a calibrated multiview ECG abnormality-screening model on embedded hardware for low-cost, on-device screening [U1].
Hybrid weather prediction
Contributed to a WRF–deep learning lightning-prediction model for hotspots in Bangladesh, funded by the Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of Bangladesh.
Atmospheric simulation
Simulated tropical cyclones with WRF and analysed their dynamical parameters.
Numerical and reduced-order modelling
Wrote a shallow-water-equation solver in Fortran; reconstructed flow fields with proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) and dynamic mode decomposition (DMD).
Multiphysics simulation
COMSOL studies of flow past multiple cylinders, heat transfer, structural response and Darcy porous-media transport; SolidWorks motion study of a slider-crank mechanism.
