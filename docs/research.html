Research

Physics-informed and operator-learning methods for the modelling, control and inverse analysis of nonlinear systems governed by partial differential equations.

Research Interests

Scientific machine learning
Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), DeepONet and Fourier neural operators, hybrid numerical–learning methods.
Learning-based control
PDE-constrained control, deep reinforcement learning, control barrier functions for safe control.
Inverse problems
Parameter estimation, identifiability, uncertainty quantification, inference from sparse data.
Embedded systems and robotics
On-device deployment of scientific ML models for control and biomedical screening; vision-based teleoperation.
Computational modelling
CFD and heat transfer, atmospheric and environmental simulation.
