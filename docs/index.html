About

I am a Lecturer in Mathematics at the Canadian University of Bangladesh and a Research Assistant at the Institute for Advanced Research, United International University. I received my B.Sc. (Honours) and M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Dhaka.

My research is in scientific machine learning and computational mathematics. I develop physics-informed and operator-learning methods for the modelling, control and inverse analysis of nonlinear systems governed by partial differential equations. This includes data-efficient surrogate modelling, reinforcement learning for constrained control, and uncertainty-aware parameter estimation. Applications include environmental, thermal-fluid, energy and biomedical systems, as well as deploying learned models on embedded and robotic platforms.

I teach undergraduate mathematics and statistics. Through the YouTube channel Functional Value I also make mathematics accessible to a wider audience.
